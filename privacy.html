Privacy policy

TechShorts Backstop is a personal tool used only by the channel owner on his own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to read the status of videos on his own channel and to change a video's visibility to Private. It does not collect, store, share or sell any other data, and it has no other users. The access token stays on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
